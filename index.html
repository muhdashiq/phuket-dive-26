Thai Met Dept (4–7 Nov, west coast): scattered to fairly widespread thundershowers, isolated heavy rain. Wednesday has the lowest rain chance of the week.

Dive plan

  1. Racha NoiMap — deeper, more current. Sea Bees lists 40 m max on this trip.
  2. Racha Noi — second site, chosen on the day for current.
  3. Racha YaiMap — easy, sheltered bays for the last dive.

Gas check: on EAN32 your MOD is 33.8 m at ppO₂ 1.4. Racha Noi can go deeper than that, so plan to your gas, not the reef.
